Privacy Policy

TAMAKIKAKU / Effective date: November 25, 2026 / 日本語版

1. Overview

Apps provided by TAMAKIKAKU ("we", "our") are designed to work entirely on your device. We do not operate servers that receive or store your personal information, and our apps do not require an account.

2. Data stored on your device

Progress, records, and settings are stored only on your device. This data is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Advertising

Some of our apps use Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob may collect information such as advertising identifiers (e.g., IDFA), device information, and app usage data in order to deliver and measure ads. Please see Google's policies for details:

  • How Google uses information for ads
  • Google Privacy Policy

Ad personalization follows your device settings and iOS tracking permission. If you purchase the ad-removal option in an app, ads that appear automatically are no longer shown in that app. Optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch remain available.

4. In-app purchases

All purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment information, such as credit card numbers.

5. When you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and the contents of your message solely to respond to your inquiry.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your information with third parties, except where required by law (and except for the advertising provider described in Section 3).

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page.
